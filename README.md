# Wayline film

A four-minute film of [Wayline](https://comp4020-final-lyclccylcq.fly.dev/), a collaborative trip planner built for ANU COMP8020. Watch it at <https://lyclccylcq.github.io/wayline-film/>.

Made with Remotion from screen recordings of the app, Mixkit stock footage, and “Letter in Orange” (酸橙色信笺) from the Arknights EP. Non-commercial course work; full credits are on the page.
